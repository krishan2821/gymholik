1. Executive Summary & Vision

Gymholik is an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform built specifically to streamline the operational, financial, and client-management lifecycles of modern fitness clubs and gyms. Operating in a high-density, multi-user environment, gym businesses require strict multi-tenant data segregation, intuitive role-specific operational interfaces, automated dues tracking, high-speed attendance check-ins, and dedicated personal training workflows.

The system eliminates manual paper logbooks, uncollected receivables, unauthorized gym access, and fractured communication between gym owners, front-desk staff, personal trainers, and gym members.

Core Value Proposition
Provide gym owners with complete financial control and anti-theft guards (audit trails & payment locks), staff with frictionless member check-in & fee collection, trainers with client accountability and weight progress notes, and members with transparent digital access.

3. Functional Requirements & Feature Matrix

3.1 Multi-Tenant Gym Onboarding & Lifecycle

  • Trial Registration: Prospective gym owners sign up with gym name, owner name, owner phone, and password. An instant 14-day free trial is activated.
  • Unique Gym Code Generation: Each tenant receives a distinct, collision-free uppercase alphanumeric gym code (e.g., TITANF4067) used by staff and trainers during registration.
  • Trial Expiration Guards: Upon trial expiry, write operations are suspended until subscription extension or payment verification by Super Admin.

3.2 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Tab Visibility

Navigation and access are strictly partitioned to prevent privilege escalation and unauthorized financial disclosure:

3.3 Member Management & Subscription Engine

  • Member Registration: Collects full name, phone number, gender, date of birth, emergency contact, photo, and initial membership plan.
  • Dynamic Plan Calculation: Start date + plan duration days calculates expiry date. Admission fee + plan price equals total payable. Supports partial payment with balance dues.
  • Membership Status Engine: Automatically derives status: ACTIVE (valid expiry), EXPIRED (expiry date in past), or DUE (outstanding balance).

3.4 Attendance & Access Control

  • Manual Quick Check-in: Real-time debounced search by name, phone, or member code with single-tap check-in.
  • Expired Access Interception: If an expired member attempts check-in, the system triggers audio/haptic warning and a modal prompt for renewal.
  • QR Code Scanning: Instant camera scanner validating member ID against gym tenant.

3.5 Financial Ledger & Payment Reconciliation

  • Payment Modes: Support for CASH, UPI, CREDIT/DEBIT CARD, and BANK TRANSFER.
  • Receipt Generation: Automated branded PDF receipt generation with breakdown of admission fee, plan fees, GST, and balance.
  • Owner Reversals: Accidental or fraudulent transactions can only be reversed by gym owners with mandatory audit reasons.

4. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

Security & Tenant Isolation

Zero tenant cross-talk. Strict ThreadLocal context combined with MongoDB query injection ensures gym data is 100% isolated. Passwords hashed with BCrypt (strength 12).

Latency & Responsiveness

API response time < 150ms at p95. App tab switching instantaneous with client-side React Query caching and zero UI layout shifts.

Offline & Fault Resilience

Cached member directories for offline search. Background synchronization when network connection is restored.

Auditability & Compliance

Immutable audit logging for staff creations, payment reversals, plan alterations, and member status overrides.

5. Success Metrics & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  • Check-in Speed: Average time from front-desk member arrival to check-in confirmation < 3 seconds.
  • Fee Recovery Rate: Reduce gym uncollected dues by > 35% within 60 days of platform adoption.
  • Platform Retention: Monthly tenant renewal rate > 94% post-trial period.
